Bread Crumbs Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Overview

Bread Crumbs is made by AppJoy. We don't have accounts or servers, and we don't sell your data. Your paths, pins, photos and settings are stored only on your iPhone.

Location

Bread Crumbs uses your location to record your trail, mark your start point and point you back to it. Your trail never leaves your phone unless you choose to share or export it.

Weather and Directions

To show weather, severe weather alerts and walking directions, your approximate location is sent to Apple (Apple Weather and Apple Maps). Apple's privacy policy applies to that data.

Camera, Photos and Microphone

Used only when you add a photo or video to a pin. Media is stored on your phone and is saved to your photo library only if you ask.

Contacts

If you choose an emergency contact, their name and phone number are stored on your phone. Bread Crumbs only messages them when you tap to send a text yourself.

Motion and Fitness

Used to count your steps along a path. Step counts stay on your phone.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. Premium removes ads. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Purchases

Premium is purchased through Apple, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. We never see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in your Apple Account settings.

Apple Watch and Lock Screen

While a path is running, your iPhone shares the path's name, start point and distance with your own Apple Watch and shows them on your Lock Screen. This information travels only between your own devices.

Children

Bread Crumbs is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app. Questions? Email appjoy.breadcrumbs@gmail.com.
