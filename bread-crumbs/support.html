Bread Crumbs Support

Never lose your way back.

Need help or have an idea?
Email appjoy.breadcrumbs@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

My trail has gaps, or stops when my phone is locked

Set Bread Crumbs' location access to Always: open the iPhone Settings app → Apps → Bread Crumbs → Location → Always. With "While Using the App", iOS pauses the trail when the screen locks.

A trail appeared while I was sitting still

GPS is less precise indoors. Bread Crumbs ignores most of this drift, but a few stray dots can appear inside buildings. Trails outdoors are much more accurate.

I ended a path before I got back

Open the path and tap Guide Me Back. You get a compass arrow and walking directions to where the path started, even after it has ended.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open Bread Crumbs → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In Bread Crumbs, go to Settings → Manage Subscription. Or open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Bread Crumbs Premium. A lifetime purchase doesn't renew and needs no canceling.

The Apple Watch app says it can't reach my iPhone

The watch app works together with Bread Crumbs on your iPhone, which does the recording. Keep your iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on, then open the watch app again. The watch app is part of Premium.

Weather alerts

Severe weather alerts come from Apple Weather and official weather agencies. They are not a replacement for official emergency alerts. Turn them on or off in Bread Crumbs → Settings.
