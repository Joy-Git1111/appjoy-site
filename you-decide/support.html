You Decide Support

Can't decide? Let fate do the rest.

Need help or have an idea?
Email appjoy.youdecide@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Some pickers are greyed out

Each picker works with a certain number of options: the Coin Toss needs exactly 2, Sleight of Hand takes up to 5, and the Pendulum and Darts up to 10 and 12. Turn options on or off in your list (tap the circle next to each one) and the matching pickers become available.

How do I add my own options or lists?

Open a list and type in Add an option. To make a new list, tap + on the home screen. Options you type yourself always stay exactly as you wrote them.

I don't see new ideas after an update

New starter ideas are added to the end of their list, switched off. Scroll down in the list and tap the circle to turn them on.

Shaking the Mystery Ball doesn't work

Give your phone a few firm shakes, then hold it still — the answer appears when the shaking stops. You can also tap the ball or the Ask the Ball button.

I don't hear any sounds

Check that Sounds is on in You Decide → Settings, and that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open You Decide → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium. Premium is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open You Decide → Settings (the gear on the home screen). You can choose a color theme, light or dark mode, and one of ten languages.
