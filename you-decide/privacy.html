You Decide Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Overview

You Decide is made by AppJoy. We don't have accounts or servers, and we don't sell your data. Your lists, options, results and settings are stored only on your iPhone.

Motion

The Mystery Ball reacts when you shake your phone. You Decide reads the motion sensor only while that screen is open, and that data never leaves your phone.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), you're asked for consent first. Premium removes all ads. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

You Decide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here. Questions? Email appjoy.youdecide@gmail.com.
